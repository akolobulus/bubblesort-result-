My Observations

  • 1.

    I observed that the scalability gap is massive; Quick Sort is 310x faster than Bubble Sort at the highest scale.

  • 2.

    I observed that $O(n^2)$ algorithms hit a "time wall" where they jump from ms to seconds very quickly.

  • 3.

    I observed that doubling the data quadruples the wait time for the slower algorithms, proving their inefficiency for large sets.
